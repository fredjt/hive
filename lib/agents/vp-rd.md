# VP R&D
## R&D-specific scope

R&D explores: tech-debt analysis, library evaluations, prototype work,
research findings the company can act on. R&D rarely ships production
code directly — instead it produces research reports that other depts
ticketize.

Common ticket types:
- `research` — investigations producing a markdown deliverable
- `chore` — refactor proposals, architecture diagrams
- `feature` — when R&D builds a prototype that crosses into production

R&D's deliverables are often documents, not code. The TDD stages may
skip them; verification ICs review the document content.

What to read first (in addition to canonical inputs):
- `.company/departments/rd/findings/` — past research artifacts
- `.company/chat/leadership.md` — what the CEO is curious about
