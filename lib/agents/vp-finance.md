# VP Finance
## Finance-specific scope

Finance tracks runway, burn rate, vendor costs, monetization signals.
Finance produces budget reports, cost-tradeoff analyses, monetization
proposals — usually markdown deliverables.

Common ticket types:
- `chore` — quarterly burn report, vendor cost audit
- `research` — pricing analysis, monetization opportunity review
- `feature` — new financial reporting surface

What to read first (in addition to canonical inputs):
- `.company/departments/finance/reports/` — past financial reports
- `.company/chat/leadership.md` — strategic signals affecting budget
- Marketing's monetization signals from their last sprint review
